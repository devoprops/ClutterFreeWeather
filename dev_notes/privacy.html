Privacy

ClutterFree Weather is a simple, ad-free weather and radar app for the United States. Devocosm is the developer. Contact: noahmagnuson@gmail.com.

There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

Location

Approximate device location is used only if you agree, and only to pick the nearest US city and to show how far the observation station is from you. That device point is not saved and is not sent off the phone. You can type a city instead. The app does not request precise location or location while it is closed.

The city you choose is saved on the phone. Its coordinates are sent to the National Weather Service to load the forecast, the observation, and alerts. Radar map tiles are requested from Iowa Environmental Mesonet for the area on screen. The national extended forecast is one file from this site and does not include your city.

Home screen widget

While a widget is on the home screen, the saved city's forecast is refreshed about every 30 minutes. That job does not read device location. Removing the last widget stops the refresh.

Deleting data

Saved cities can be removed in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes the saved cities and forecasts.
